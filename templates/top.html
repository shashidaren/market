how the probability is calculated & what it does not mean
  • One stock. The tab ranks a shortlist, then shows only the highest-probability name — the /movers tab is the one that shows five.
  • Universe. Stocks the insider signal engine scored in the window, plus stocks mentioned in recently scored headlines. Only a shortlist is priced, because Yahoo is rate-limited and shared with the FX, indices and Telegram jobs on this server.
  • Probability. Each stock's own 1-year daily history gives the empirical frequency of a ≥3.0% day (close vs previous close). That base rate is multiplied by today's conditions: volume expansion, volatility regime vs its 1-year median, position in the 52-week range, news mentions, insider conviction and the move already in progress. The hero card shows the arithmetic, so the number is auditable.
  • Not calibrated. The multiplier weights are hand-set heuristics; there is no back-test behind them. Treat the score as a ranking aid and a prompt to look at the chart — not as a win rate.
  • Prices: Yahoo Finance daily bars for XXXX.KL, delayed ~15 min, shared cache with /movers (≤10 min during Bursa hours, ≤60 min otherwise). Not an official Bursa feed.
  • News data is as good as the scorer's company matching (tickers.py). If a company name is missing from the registry, its headlines never reach this tab — run python3 tickers.py suggest.
  • Informational only, not investment advice.
