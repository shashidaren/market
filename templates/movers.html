data notes & limits
  • Prices: Yahoo Finance daily bars for XXXX.KL, delayed ~15 min. Not tick data and not an official Bursa feed.
  • Caching: each stock is fetched at most once per ~10 min during Bursa hours (~60 min otherwise), shared by every open browser. If Yahoo rate-limits the server, the tab pauses fetching and shows cached data.
  • Ranking: only stocks the insider signal engine scored > 0 in the chosen window are ranked (same rule as the insider tab). Capped at 5 to keep Yahoo traffic low.
  • Markers sit on the trade date (date of change). Filings are usually published 1–3+ days later, so part of the move can happen before the public knows.
  • Net shares combine director (S219) and substantial holder (S138) filings, de-duplicated per person/date/size. Deemed/indirect interests can still report one deal more than once, so the numbers can differ from the insider tab, which counts director filings only.
  • Insider avg price uses only filings that state a per-share price. Method tags (open-mkt, off-mkt, esos…) are best-effort guesses from the filing text.
  • Informational only, not investment advice.
