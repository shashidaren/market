where this data comes from & what it does not mean
  • Source. Bursa Malaysia Changes in Sub. S-hldr's Int (Section 138) filings, collected by the announcements pipeline into bursa_announcements + bursa_insider_details. The same tables the insider tab reads — no extra cron job, no Yahoo calls.
  • Who counts as an institution. The filer name on the filing (person_name) is matched against a small fund catalog: EPF/KWSP, KWAP, Amanah Saham Bumiputera / Amanah Saham Nasional (which file through ASNB), plus PNB, Khazanah, Tabung Haji, LTAT, Urusharta Jamaah, ValueCap and MOF Inc as optional chips. Short codes only match as a whole name, so "ASB Resources Sdn Bhd" is not mistaken for Amanah Saham Bumiputera.
  • Most recent first = newest date of change (the trade date), falling back to the filing date. Filings usually land 1–3 days after the trade, so the "filed" line shows the lag.
  • Dedupe. Filings are keyed by (fund, stock, date, side, shares) — a re-filed or corrected notice is counted once.
  • Values (RM) are only shown when the filing states a per-share price. A filing that quotes a total amount ("RM1,250,000") is not converted, so the number is never invented.
  • Gaps are possible. S138 notices are only in this database if the collector saw them; the collector pages through recent announcements, so a very busy day can leave holes. Cross-check on Bursa before acting.
  • Informational only, not investment advice.
